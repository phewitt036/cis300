Questions people actually ask

Six questions, answered straight. The ones about trust come first.

Where does the data come from?

Your league's settings and rosters come from Yahoo Fantasy's official API. Weekly point projections and injury status come from Sleeper, and news headlines come from the free public feeds at RotoWire and ESPN. Nothing is taken from paywalled sites.

Does it know my league's scoring?

Yes. It reads every scoring rule from Yahoo and re-scores each projection with those exact rules, including PPR. Our league even scores field goals by yardage instead of by distance, and the tool handles that too. Roster slots are read the same way.

What does it get wrong?

It is only as good as the projection underneath it, and news is collected on a schedule, so a late scratch can slip past it. The How It Decides page lists every known blind spot.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The data sources are free, and the whole thing runs on a small computer in my house. There are no ads and no subscription.

Can it see my team?

It reads the rosters in the one Yahoo league it was built for. It cannot set your lineup or make trades — you still make every move yourself — and it is not open to other leagues yet.

Who made this?

Patrick Hewitt, a student at Arizona State University. I built the advisor for my own league and this site for CIS 300. The About page has the whole story.
